## Who this is for

A finance professional (10+ years; controlling, reporting, and
finance-management scope across mid-size international companies) seeking
a finance-manager / controller seat in the Zurich region. Registered with
the RAV, which means two things for scoring: a steady stream of REAL,
suitable applications matters every single month, and "suitable work" is
read a notch more broadly than a purely opportunistic search would.

## Location tiers (score by tier)

1. **Zurich region** — ZH, Zug, Aargau east, Winterthur; top scores live here
2. **Rest of Switzerland** — commutable or relocation-plausible
3. **Remote — Switzerland/EU timezone**
4. **DACH neighbors, in-person** — Germany/Austria/Liechtenstein; below
   the Swiss options, note the cross-border implications in watch_out
- Anything else in-person: score 1-2 unless fully remote

## Title universe

**Core targets**: Finance Manager, Controller, Senior Controller,
Financial Controller, Head of Controlling, Leiter/in Finanzen,
Finanzverantwortliche/r.

**Same level, different words**: Business Controller, Group Controller,
Finance Business Partner, Head of Accounting, Leiter Rechnungswesen.

**One notch down but keeps the search moving** (score normally; add
watch_out "a step down in scope - weigh against the monthly quota"):
Senior Accountant, Accounting Manager, Treuhand roles with controlling
scope.

**Engagement-shaped** (boost): interim controller, fractional finance
lead, maternity-cover finance manager (common in CH and genuinely
winnable from unemployment).

**Left-field wildcards** (flag distinctly, don't bury): finance roles at
scale-ups leaving the founder-does-the-books stage, Gemeinde/canton
finance administration, NGO finance leads.

## Language rule

Can apply in German and English. Ads in French or Italian score at most 2
unless explicitly open to German/English applications - name the language
in watch_out.

## Salary rule — apply ONLY when the posting states compensation

Floors exist by region; a stated figure below the floor (in the salary
field OR the description) scores 1 with the number quoted in watch_out.
Swiss postings rarely state salary - silence is normal, score normally
and say nothing about it.

## Workload rule

Target 80-100%. 60-80% scores at most 3 with the percentage named in
watch_out; below 60% scores 1.

## Restricted hiring — hard rule

Postings reserved for (or strongly preferring) a group the candidate does
not belong to (e.g. Swiss-citizenship-required federal roles, IV/social
reintegration placements) score 1, with the restriction named plainly in
watch_out. Legitimate practice; just not a winnable seat.

## Scoring calibration notes

- Most Swiss mid-size employers have a thin public footprint; absence of
  evidence is low-signal, not a red flag.
- Job-Room ads are frequently syndicated from jobs.ch/jobup.ch - identical
  ads under different sources are the same seat, not two chances.
- Skeptical of "Controller" titles that are pure bookkeeping: no
  analysis/partnering scope, score down a notch and say so.
- The unemployment gap is a fact, not a stain: frame readiness and
  availability (immediate start is an ASSET in CH hiring) rather than
  apology.

## Who this is for

A bioengineer finishing a PhD in Biomedical Engineering (AO Research
Institute Davos and the University of Basel; Marie Skłodowska-Curie
doctoral fellow, defence expected autumn 2026), with an MSc and BSc in
Biological Engineering from Instituto Superior Técnico, Lisbon. Four-plus
years in tissue engineering and regenerative medicine: 2D and 3D primary
human cell culture (bone-marrow stromal cells, articular and nasal
chondrocytes), hydrogel biomaterials (GelMA synthesis, rheology,
mechanical testing), bioreactor-driven mechanical stimulation, histology,
immunofluorescence, RNAscope, confocal and light-sheet microscopy. GMP
exposure from manufacturing an engineered cartilage graft in a GMP
workflow at the University of Basel and from implementing a GLP-adapted
version of that protocol at the home institute. Earlier: bioprocess
design (a GMP-compliant recombinant-protein production process, mass and
energy balances, economics) and microfabrication (microfluidic chips, CNC
micro-milling, 3D printing).

Moving from academia to industry. Registered with the RAV in Graubünden
(advisor appointment pending; generic RAV rules apply until then): a
steady stream of REAL, suitable applications matters every month, and
"suitable work" is read a notch more broadly than a purely opportunistic
search would. She will relocate anywhere in the tiers below.

## Language rule

Applies in **English only**. Portuguese native, English C2, Spanish B1,
German A2.

- Ads in English: score normally.
- Ads in German at international employers (big pharma, CDMOs, medtech
  multinationals, research institutes) where English is the working
  language: score normally, but watch_out must say "ad in German - confirm
  English applications are accepted; apply in English".
- Ads that require fluent German ("sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse",
  "Deutsch C1", "Muttersprache") or that are plainly German-speaking
  workplaces (regional labs, cantonal hospitals, Laborant seats): score
  at most 2 and name the language requirement.
- French or Italian ads: at most 2 unless explicitly open to English.
- Dutch, Danish, Austrian and German ads in tier 4: same rule; note that
  Dutch and Danish workplaces are usually English-speaking for
  scientists.

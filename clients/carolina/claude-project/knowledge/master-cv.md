## Profile

Bioengineer with 4+ years in tissue engineering and regenerative
medicine, finishing a PhD in Biomedical Engineering (AO Research Institute
Davos and University of Basel, Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral fellow;
defence expected autumn 2026). Hands-on with 2D and 3D primary human cell
culture, hydrogel biomaterials, bioreactor-driven mechanical stimulation,
histology, immunofluorescence, RNAscope, confocal and light-sheet
microscopy, and biochemical assays. Has manufactured a cell-based
cartilage graft inside a GMP workflow and implemented a GLP-adapted
version of the protocol in a research setting. Engineering foundation in
bioprocess design and microfabrication. Targeting a first industry seat in
R&D, process development or manufacturing science.

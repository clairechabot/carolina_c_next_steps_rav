# Onboarding — build your Next Chapter, one step at a time

Do these in order. Steps 1–5 get a real email flowing with the built-in
example search (a finance professional in the Zurich region); steps 6–7
make it yours; step 8 + 11 are the RAV compliance heart of this edition;
9 is an optional power-up.

| 4 | [Turn on the web edition](04-web-edition.md) | 10 min |

| 11 | [The RAV compliance layer](11-rav-compliance.md) | 20 min |
| 12 | [Operators: several clients from one template](12-multi-client.md) | 15 min |

**The one tip that matters**: do all of this with Claude open in another
tab (claude.ai). Confused by a step? Paste the step and what you're seeing
into Claude and ask. That's the intended way to use this guide.

Two roles appear throughout: the **job seeker** (who the newsletter is
for) and the **operator** (who's setting it up — often a family member).
